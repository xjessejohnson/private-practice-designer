Marriage & Family Therapy – Christian Counseling Center

Top-Rated Counseling services for individuals, marriages, and families.

Maybe you can’t name it yet. Just a weight that’s gotten heavier. A relationship that feels further away than it should. A version of your life that stopped fitting somewhere along the way and you’re not sure when it happened or how to get back.

Whatever it is, it matters. And the fact that you’re here, looking for something better, says a lot about who you are.

We are Old Way Counseling and Consulting – the region’s highest-rated counseling practice. For more than 30 years, we’ve walked with people through some of the hardest seasons a human being can face. And we’ve watched them come out the other side.

Not just surviving. Actually living.

What you’re carrying isn’t a character flaw.

It’s not proof that you’re too far gone or that things can’t be different. It’s a signal – one that brought you here. And that means something.

At Old Way Counseling, we don’t fit you into someone else’s framework or hand you a generic plan. We start by getting to know you – how you think, what you’ve been through, what you actually want your life to look like. Then we build something around that. No one-size-fits-all. No assumptions about your story before we’ve heard it.

We believe that God has a life of purpose, power, and passion for you. Take the next step and reach out to us by calling (555) 019-2834 today!

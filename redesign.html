Something brought you here today. Counseling and therapy in Oklahoma City and Edmond — for the moments that matter most.

Maybe you can't name it yet. Just a weight that's gotten heavier. A relationship that feels further away than it should. A version of your life that stopped fitting somewhere along the way and you're not sure when it happened or how to get back. Whatever it is, it matters. And the fact that you're here, looking for something better, says a lot about who you are. We are Old Way Counseling and Consulting – Oklahoma's highest-rated counseling practice. For more than 30 years, we've walked with people through some of the hardest seasons a human being can face. And we've watched them come out the other side. Not just surviving. Actually living. Read More ›

What you're carrying isn't a character flaw. It's not proof that you're too far gone or that things can't be different. It's a signal – one that brought you here. And that means something. At Old Way Counseling, we don't fit you into someone else's framework or hand you a generic plan. We start by getting to know you – how you think, what you've been through, what you actually want your life to look like. Then we build something around that. No one-size-fits-all. No assumptions about your story before we've heard it. We work with individuals, couples, kids, and families across Oklahoma City and Edmond who are navigating life's toughest challenges. We believe that God has a life of purpose, power, and passion for you. We consider it an honor to partner with God and with you in prayer and faith to reach your goals.
